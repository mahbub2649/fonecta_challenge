# fonecta_challenge

Connect Suggests — Customer Approves
Company - Fonecta


Description

Imagine walking to a café because the internet says it is open—only to find the door locked. For local businesses, a forgotten update to online information can mean disappointed customers, missed revenue and lost trust. Keeping every digital channel accurate is important, but busy owners rarely have time to manage it all. Fonecta invites you to reimagine business-information management as a proactive service. Instead of waiting for a customer to log in and make changes, what if an intelligent system could notice an issue, explain a suggested update and let the business owner respond in seconds? Build around a simple but powerful idea: detect what needs attention, recommend a sensible action and keep the human in control. This challenge brings together agentic workflows, customer communication, data quality, automation and product design. A strong prototype could show how software stops being a dashboard people must remember to use—and starts becoming an assistant that quietly helps thousands of local businesses stay discoverable and reliable.
